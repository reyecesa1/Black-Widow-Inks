The Artist

A Journey Through Ink

For over a decade, I've channeled my struggles, my pain, and my healing into every piece I create. What began as an escape became my salvation.

Each tattoo represents not just artistic expression, but a piece of someone's journey - their pain, their hope, their transformation. I believe in art that carries weight, that means something beyond aesthetics.

"The needle doesn't just deposit ink into skin - it releases the stories we carry within. My art is my testimony that beauty can emerge from brokenness."

- The Artist

FAQ

How do I prepare?

Stay hydrated, eat a meal beforehand, avoid alcohol for 24 hours. Wear loose clothing covering the area.

Does it hurt?

Yes, but tolerance varies. Numbing cream available upon request (+$15).

Aftercare?

You'll receive a full aftercare kit and instructions. Keep clean, moisturise, avoid sun/swimming for 2 weeks.

Can I bring a friend?

One guest is welcome. No additional visitors for hygiene and space.

Rescheduling?

Contact at least 48h before. Deposits forfeited for last‑minute cancellations (under 24h).

Cover‑ups?

Yes – send a clear photo during consultation for assessment.
