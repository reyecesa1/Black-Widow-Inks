FAQ

How do I prepare for my appointment?

Stay hydrated, eat a good meal beforehand, and avoid alcohol for 24 hours. Wear loose clothing covering the area.

Does it hurt?

Yes, but pain tolerance varies. We use numbing cream upon request (additional $15). Most clients describe it as a scratching sensation.

How do I care for my new tattoo?

You'll receive a full aftercare kit and written instructions. Keep it clean, moisturise, and avoid direct sun/swimming for 2 weeks.

Can I bring a friend?

Of course – one guest is welcome. For hygiene and space, no additional visitors please.

What if I need to reschedule?

Contact me at least 48 hours before your appointment. Deposits are forfeited for last‑minute cancellations (under 24h).

Do you offer cover‑ups?

Yes, I specialise in cover‑up designs. Send a clear photo during consultation for assessment.
